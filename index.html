BELGIUM GEO PROJECT

When we were assigned this project, we wanted to look beyond just geography facts no one talks about. It gives no importance to the beauty of Belgium, such as their cross-section of culture, heavy industry and high-tech agriculture. We wanted to shine a light on the most underrated country resting in Europe.

"To me, Belgium remains one of Europe's best kept secrets."

Before we dive into the eye-opening data, ranging from Antwerp's global diamond market to the science behind the best fries in the world, let's take a look at what makes Belgium, well; Belgium!
